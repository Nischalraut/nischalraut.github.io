Final Year Project · Experimental Aerodynamics

Subsonic Wind Tunnel

Designed & built open-jet tunnel with gust generator. Turbulent Intensity 0.88% validated experimentally.

Designed and fabricated an open-jet wind tunnel featuring a custom gust generator with oscillating vanes. The system was validated experimentally to achieve a turbulence intensity of 0.88%, providing a controlled environment for unsteady aerodynamic analysis.
